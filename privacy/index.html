Who this covers

Hermes Star Personal (Star) is a privately operated assistant for personal use. This policy covers its authorized user and these public information pages. The assistant is not a public service.

Connected information

With authorization, Star can read connected Google information, create email drafts, send email, and create, update, or delete calendar events and tasks. File, document, and spreadsheet access remains read-only. Actions follow the user’s instructions and granted permissions.

A separate notes connection can read notes, checklists, and labels, and create or update notes, checklist items, pinned status, and archived status under the user’s instructions and granted permissions. It does not support deleting or sharing notes.

Processing & use

Connected information is used to answer questions, summarize content, and carry out authorized requests. Requests, retrieved content, and relevant conversation context may be sent to external AI service providers for processing; this is not an on-device-only service. Provider handling is subject to their terms and settings.

The operator does not sell connected information, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Star's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage & retention

Access credentials are stored privately on the operator's device. Retrieved information, conversations, and saved context may be retained in local storage and private backups until removed by the operator. Backup copies require separate removal. There is no fixed retention period; external providers apply their own retention policies and settings.

Control & revocation

Google OAuth access can be revoked through Google Account third-party connections. Other connections must be disconnected separately by the operator.

Disconnecting an account does not erase previously retained information. The operator can remove local history, saved context, credentials, and backup copies separately. Use the support contact below for access or deletion requests.

This public website

This is an informational website, not an assistant interface. It does not publish connected account data.

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process IP addresses and technical request information for delivery and security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This site adds no analytics, scripts, forms, or cookies. Links to other services lead to sites governed by their respective privacy policies.

Contact

The authorized user can contact the operator using the support email shown on the application's Google consent screen.
